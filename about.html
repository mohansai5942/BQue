About

A small team that answers to you — like our assistants answer to your customers.

BrandiQue Voice AI is a service from BrandiQue Web Solutions, based in Visakhapatnam, India. We design, build and support custom AI voice assistants for businesses that depend on phone calls.

We started this service for a simple reason: most small and mid-size businesses lose enquiries to missed calls, busy lines and after-hours silence — not because they don't care, but because people can't be on the phone all day. A well-built voice assistant fixes exactly that, without replacing the humans customers trust.

How we're different

  • Done-for-you, not do-it-yourself. There's no portal to learn and no dashboard you must babysit. We build it, we run it, we support it.
  • Your language first. We work in Telugu, Hindi, English and other Indian languages — configured for the customers you actually serve.
  • Honest scoping. If a voice assistant isn't the right fix for your problem, we'll tell you that instead of selling you one.

Who runs this

Mohan Rao, founder of BrandiQue Web Solutions — web, AI automation and now voice AI for local businesses. Every project is handled directly, not handed to a support queue.
