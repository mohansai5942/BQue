How it works

Three steps. No software for you to learn.

You bring knowledge of your business. We bring everything else.

1

Discuss your business need

We start with a conversation — not a sales pitch. We map out:

  • What your callers usually ask for
  • Which calls should be automated, and which must reach a human
  • Your services, timings, pricing and booking rules
  • The language and tone your customers expect

You end this step with a clear, written plan of what the assistant will and won't do — before anything is built.

2

We build and test it with you

We design the conversation flow, configure the voice and language, and set up call handling, lead capture and human handover rules.

Then we test together using real scenarios from your business — tricky questions, difficult callers, edge cases. The assistant only moves forward when you approve how it sounds and behaves.

3

Launch, then ongoing support

We help connect the assistant to your phone workflow — your existing number, a new line, or after-hours coverage — and go live.

After launch we stay involved: monitoring how calls go, refining responses, and updating the assistant as your services, timings or policies change. One point of contact, no portals to manage.

Deliverables

What you get at each step

Tangible outputs — not vague promises.

Step 1

Written call plan

A document describing every call flow: what the assistant says, asks and does — plus where humans take over. You sign it off before we build.

Step 2

Tested assistant + test report

The working assistant, tested against your real scenarios, with a summary of what was tested and fixed. You approve the final version.

Step 3

Live assistant + support

Connected to your phone workflow, with call summaries from day one — plus the agreed support for updates and improvements.

What we need from you

Surprisingly little

1–2 conversations

Walk us through your business and your calls. An hour of your time is usually enough to start.

Your business details

Services, prices, timings, FAQs — whatever your team answers on the phone today.

Feedback on test calls

Listen, tell us what to change, and approve the final version. That's the whole job on your side.
