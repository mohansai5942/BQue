About usage charges — read this before you decide.
Running a voice assistant involves third-party costs: phone numbers and call charges (telecom provider), speech and AI model usage (AI providers). These are billed separately at actual cost, or bundled into a custom plan if you prefer one bill — we'll spell out exactly which option applies in your written quote. We never mark up third-party usage without telling you.
Transparency

What shapes your quote

Six things decide the price. We'll walk through each on our scoping call — nothing is a black box.

Number of call flows

One flow (e.g. appointment booking) vs. several (reception + bookings + support). More flows = more design and testing.

Languages

Single language vs. two or mixed-language speaking. Each language needs its own script review and testing.

Expected call volume

Higher volume needs more robust telephony and AI capacity — and affects third-party usage costs directly.

Integrations

Calendar booking, CRM updates, WhatsApp messaging, sheets logging — each integration is scoped separately.

Support level

Launch-only support vs. monthly reviews, script updates and priority response times.

Timeline

Standard build vs. rush delivery. We'll always tell you what's realistic before committing.

📋 What every quote includes, in writing

  1. Scope — exactly which call flows, languages and integrations are covered (and what isn't).
  2. One-time setup cost — design, build, voice configuration, testing and launch.
  3. Support terms — what's included after launch, response times, and how changes are handled.
  4. Usage estimate — expected third-party costs (phone + AI usage) at your volume, billed separately at cost unless bundled.
  5. Timeline — agreed milestones, in writing. No "soon".
