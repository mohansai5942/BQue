FAQ

Questions owners usually ask

Is this a product I install myself?

No. This is a done-for-you service — we build, configure and support the assistant for your business. There's no self-serve portal or app you need to manage.

Will callers know it's an AI?

We design assistants to be natural and helpful, and we recommend being transparent that callers are speaking with an AI assistant. Most callers care more about getting help quickly than about who — or what — is helping.

What languages can it speak?

We configure the voice and language around your customers — including Telugu, Hindi, English and other Indian languages. Tell us your audience in the demo request and we'll advise what's practical.

What happens when the AI can't handle a call?

We build escalation rules together: transfer to your number, take a callback request, or log it for follow-up. You decide where the human line is.

Do I need to change my phone number?

Not necessarily. During setup we discuss the best call-routing option for your business — in many cases your existing number keeps working as usual.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your use case and call volume. See our pricing page for how we structure quotes, or request a demo for a custom estimate.

Do you work with businesses outside Andhra Pradesh & Telangana?

Yes — discovery, build and support all happen over call and screen-share. The assistant is configured for your customers' language wherever they are.

Who owns the call recordings and data?

You do. Data handling — what's recorded, how long it's kept, who can access it — is agreed in writing before launch. See our privacy policy for the starter terms.

What if I want to change what the assistant says later?

That's part of the service. New services, changed timings, seasonal offers — send us the update and we reconfigure the assistant. Ongoing support plans cover this.

Do I need new hardware or a new phone system?

Usually not. During setup we work out call routing with your existing number and equipment. If anything extra is ever needed, we'll tell you before you spend a rupee.

Can it do bulk promotional calling?

For service-style outreach (reminders, follow-ups to your own enquiries) yes. For promotional bulk calling in India, DLT registration and consent rules apply — we'll guide you on what's compliant before any campaign.
