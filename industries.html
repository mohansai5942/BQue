Industries & use cases

Built for businesses that run on phone calls.

Every industry below gets a custom call flow — your services, your questions, your booking rules. These are starting points, not templates.

Real estate

Never lose a site-visit lead

New enquiry calls get answered instantly — even at 10 PM. The assistant asks about budget, preferred location and timeline, then books a site visit and sends you the qualified details. Your agents meet ready buyers instead of chasing cold numbers.

Clinics & healthcare

A front desk that never sleeps

Appointment requests, rescheduling, directions, fee questions — handled without interrupting consultations. After hours, it takes messages and books morning slots so your day starts full.

Coaching centres

Admission enquiries, answered

Course details, fee structures, batch timings, demo class bookings — the assistant answers instantly and flags serious enquiries for your counsellors to close.

Hospitality

Reservations without the rush

Table bookings, room enquiries, event dates, directions and opening hours — handled politely even during your busiest service. Special requests get escalated to your team.

Customer support

First line, always on

Common issues resolved on the call, complaints logged with details, and complex cases handed to your team with full context — so customers never repeat themselves.

Local services

Salons, showrooms, agencies & more

Price enquiries, slot bookings, service questions — if your business starts with a phone call, we'll design a flow that fits your counter, your team and your customers.

Example call flows

What a call sounds like, step by step

Illustrative examples — your real flows are written around your business.

🏥 Clinic — appointment booking

1

Greeting & routing

"Thanks for calling Sunrise Dental — are you booking a visit, or is this about an existing appointment?"

2

Need identification

Asks what's needed — cleaning, pain, checkup — and whether it's urgent. Urgent cases jump the queue per your rules.

3

Slot offer

Offers the next free slots from your schedule: "Tomorrow we have 10:30 AM and 12 PM."

4

Confirm & remind

Books the slot, takes the name and number, sends a confirmation — and a reminder before the visit.

🏠 Real estate — lead qualification

1

Instant callback

New portal enquiry → assistant calls within minutes, while the buyer is still browsing.

2

Qualification

Budget range, preferred location, timeline, loan vs. outright — the four answers your agents need.

3

Site visit booking

Serious buyers get a slot; brochure and location go over WhatsApp mid-call.

4

Agent handover

Your agent receives: name, number, budget, timeline and visit slot — no cold calling.

🎓 Coaching centre — admission enquiry

1

Course mapping

"Which exam are you preparing for?" — matches the caller to the right course and batch.

2

Fee & batch answers

Answers fee, duration and batch-timing questions exactly as you've defined them.

3

Demo class booking

Books a free demo class and notes the student's subjects and target exam.

4

Counsellor shortlist

Serious enquiries land with your counsellors; casual browsers get course details on WhatsApp.
