In practice

How these services actually work

Not feature-list fluff — here's what we set up for you, step by step.

⚡ Instant lead callback

A new enquiry shouldn't wait hours for a response. We connect your lead sources to an outbound call flow:

  1. Lead arrives — from your website form, Instagram/WhatsApp enquiry, or a shared sheet. We agree on which sources trigger a call.
  2. Assistant calls within minutes — introduces your business, answers first questions, and qualifies the lead (budget, need, timeline — whatever matters to you).
  3. You get structured details — name, number, requirement and interest level, delivered where you actually read them (WhatsApp/message).
  4. Hot leads get booked — site visit, appointment or callback scheduled on the same call, per your availability rules.

📢 Bulk & reminder calling

For reaching a list — appointment reminders, fee-due nudges, event invitations:

  1. You share the list and the message — we help word it so it sounds human, not spammy.
  2. Calls go out in batches — each conversation adapts (confirm, reschedule, or take a message).
  3. You get a report — who answered, who confirmed, who asked for a callback, who didn't pick up.
  4. Compliance first — promotional campaigns need DLT registration and consent under Indian telecom rules. We guide you through it before anything goes out.

💬 Mid-call actions

The assistant doesn't just talk — it does things while the caller is still on the line:

  1. Send details instantly — price list, brochure, location pin or booking link over WhatsApp/SMS during the call.
  2. Book and confirm — appointment locked in, confirmation sent before the call ends.
  3. Log everything — the conversation summary lands in your inbox/sheet the moment the call finishes.

🧠 Summaries, memory & handover

Every call ends with perfect notes — and the assistant remembers repeat callers:

  1. Structured summary per call — who called, what they wanted, what was decided, what needs follow-up.
  2. Caller memory — a returning customer gets a "welcome back" instead of repeating their history.
  3. Human handover with context — when a call escalates, you (or your team) get the full background first, so the customer never repeats themselves.
